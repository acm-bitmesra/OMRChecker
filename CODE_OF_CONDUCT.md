# Code of Conduct

We want OMR Checker to be welcoming, respectful, and collaborative for contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior
- Be kind, respectful, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback gracefully.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and curiosity.
- Respect privacy; never share student data or private information.
- Keep discussions inclusive and on topic.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Abusive language in project spaces.
- Doxxing or sharing private information without consent.
- Publishing credentials, student records, or confidential scans.
- Deliberately disrupting project discussions or contributions.

Report concerns privately to maintainers. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and restrict participation when necessary. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.
