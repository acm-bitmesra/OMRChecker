# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not disclose vulnerabilities publicly before maintainers have a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate them.

Contact repository maintainers privately through the GitHub organization contact or GitHub's private vulnerability reporting feature, when enabled. Include affected component/version, impact, reproduction steps, and suggested mitigation if known.

Do not include real student records, credentials, or private scan images in reports.

## Priority areas
- Cross-organization data access.
- Unauthorized access to scans, answers, or results.
- Upload parsing and resource exhaustion.
- Authentication/session weaknesses.
- Secret exposure and insecure signed URLs.
- Result tampering or audit-log bypass.

Maintainers will acknowledge, investigate, coordinate remediation, and communicate a fix. No response SLA is promised until maintainers publish one.
